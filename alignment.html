Alignment Chart

Click anywhere on the chart to drop a point for where you think something (or someone) falls, and give it a short label. Every visitor's points are public and permanent — you can remove your own points, but not anyone else's.

Setup needed: this page needs a Firebase project configured before it can save points. See the firebaseConfig placeholder near the top of the <script type="module"> block in alignment.html.
